OUR
ARTISTS

The works displayed in our gallery span centuries and styles. Get to know the painters whose pieces have found a home with us.

Vincent
van Gogh

A Dutch Post-Impressionist painter whose vivid colors and emotional honesty made him one of history's most influential artists. His turbulent inner life fueled works of extraordinary energy and feeling.

1853 – 1890

Dutch · Post-Impressionism

Frida
Kahlo

A Mexican painter celebrated for her deeply personal self-portraits and her unflinching exploration of identity, pain, and resilience. Her bold imagery continues to resonate across cultures worldwide.

1907 – 1954

Mexican · Surrealism

Claude
Monet

The father of French Impressionism, Monet devoted his life to capturing fleeting light and atmosphere. His water lily series, painted in his legendary garden at Giverny, remains among art's most beloved achievements.

1840 – 1926

French · Impressionism

Georgia
O'Keeffe

An American modernist known for her large-scale paintings of flowers, New Mexico landscapes, and bones. Her ability to find the monumental within the intimate changed the course of American art.

1887 – 1986

American · Modernism

Pablo
Picasso

A Spanish painter and co-founder of Cubism whose restless reinvention of form, perspective, and style defined the 20th century avant-garde. Few artists have shaped modern visual culture so profoundly.

1881 – 1973

Spanish · Cubism

Johannes
Vermeer

A Dutch Golden Age master renowned for his quiet domestic interiors bathed in delicate window light. Works like Girl with a Pearl Earring display an intimacy and technical mastery rarely equalled.

1632 – 1675

Dutch · Baroque
